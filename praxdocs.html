Compress PDF

Reduce PDF file size by re-rendering pages at a lower resolution. Choose a quality preset to balance file size against visual fidelity.

Compression Quality
📱
Screen
Smallest file. Good for email & web sharing.
~72 DPI · JPEG 40%
📖
eBook
Balanced. Readable on screen, much smaller.
~96 DPI · JPEG 60%
🖨️
Printer
Good print quality with moderate compression.
~150 DPI · JPEG 80%
🎨
Prepress
Near-lossless. Light compression, highest quality.
~200 DPI · JPEG 92%
Pages are re-rendered via PDF.js and re-embedded as compressed images. Text-only PDFs may not shrink significantly — this works best on PDFs containing images or scans.
